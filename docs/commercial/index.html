Single-ply

EPDM

Black rubber for irregular roofs and ballasted decks; excellent hail history.

  • 60/90-mil, seamed or adhered
  • Walkable in all seasons
  • Simple, repairable detailing
Asphaltic

Modified Bitumen

Torch or cold-adhesive SBS for roofs with heavy foot traffic and units.

  • 2-ply cap sheet, granulated
  • Puncture-resistant surface
  • Ideal under pavers & decks
Metal

Standing-Seam Retrofit

Clip-over system installed directly over an ageing metal roof — no tear-off.

  • 26-gauge panels, Kynar finish
  • Adds R-value with iso board
  • 50-yr panel life expectancy
Restoration

Fluid-applied restoration that extends a serviceable roof 10-15 more years.

  • Reflective, ponding-rated
  • Fraction of replacement cost
